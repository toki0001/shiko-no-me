## 画像生成

Codex内蔵image_genを使用。1回生成、再生成なし。既存の暫定サムネイルを色と雰囲気の参考にした。生成後はJPEG変換のみ行い、描画内容は編集していない。

```text
Asset type: ZEN Study web app contest submission thumbnail / Japanese product cover
Primary request: Create a polished, simple, wide conceptual cover image introducing the thinking-notes app 「思考の芽」. It must read instantly at thumbnail size as an idea branching into choices and a person deciding among them. This is an editorial concept visual, not a screenshot or a depiction of a real app screen.
Input images: Image 1 is a reference only for the existing product's warm paper-like off-white, deep forest green, pale sage, charcoal text, restrained thin green connector lines, and quiet digital stationery feel. Do not reproduce its screenshot, labels, controls, layout, or sample content.
Scene/backdrop: Spacious warm off-white paper-like field with barely visible fine dot texture, clean premium digital stationery aesthetic.
Subject: A central rounded white thought card branches via smooth, elegant deep-green lines to exactly three smaller white choice cards. One choice is clearly marked with a compact forest-green pill 「採用」; the other two have small calm charcoal/sage labels 「保留」 and 「見送り」. A tiny understated fresh sprout with two sage leaves is organically integrated into one branch junction; it is a subtle accent, not the main subject. Card text is sparse and easy to read, using only brief authentic labels such as 「考え」「別の案」「判断の理由」. No more cards or labels.
Style/medium: High-quality flat editorial illustration with precise typography and clean vector-like shapes, soft paper grain only, crisp edges, no glossy 3D.
Composition/framing: Wide horizontal 16:10 cover. Keep large title and subtitle grouped on the left third, vertically centered with generous breathing room. Arrange the branching card concept in the central-to-right two-thirds, balanced and uncluttered. Main title must be the strongest element; keep every object within safe margins for a thumbnail crop. No frame, no device, no window mockup.
Lighting/mood: Calm, thoughtful, optimistic, tactile, precise.
Color palette: Warm off-white, deep forest green, pale sage, charcoal; strictly restrained.
Text (verbatim): Large, perfectly accurate Japanese title 「思考の芽」. Under it, smaller but clearly legible exact subtitle 「考えを広げて、自分で選ぶ。」. On the three choice cards, use short Japanese labels only: 「考え」, 「別の案」, 「判断の理由」; statuses: 「採用」, 「保留」, 「見送り」. Render Japanese text faithfully with natural Japanese typography; do not add any other words.
Constraints: Wide landscape 16:10 aspect ratio, premium simple composition, strong readable title, clear branching structure with one selected option. Represent human choice and reflection without implying automation.
Avoid: Actual application screenshot, fake UI chrome, device or laptop mockup, browser controls, AI chat bubbles, AI robot, analytics or grades, awards, university or contest name, dates, account or cloud sync symbols, rainbow gradients, decorative clutter, ornamental typography, extra text, watermark, logo.
```
